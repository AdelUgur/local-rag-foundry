# 3D Printer Guide

The club runs one 3D printer in the workshop. It is shared, heavily used, and
easy to break, so please read this before booking.

## Before you can book

You must hold a **valid lab induction** before you may book or operate the
printer. Booking requests from members without a current induction are declined
automatically. Induction scheduling is covered in the lab safety notes.

## Booking

Bookings must be made **at least 48 hours in advance** through the workshop
calendar. Walk-up use is not possible, even when the printer is idle.

A single session may run for a maximum of **6 hours**. Longer prints must be
split across sessions or discussed with the workshop officer in advance.

If you cannot attend, cancel your booking. Two unattended bookings in a term
cost you printer access for the rest of that term.

## Filament deposit

A **100 TL** filament deposit is charged per booking. It covers the material you
use and is refunded, minus the filament actually consumed, after the session.

## Supported materials

The printer is configured for **PLA and PETG** only.

**ABS is not permitted.** It releases fumes that the workshop's ventilation
cannot handle. Bringing your own ABS filament is grounds for losing printer
access.

## Failed prints

Failed prints happen and are not held against you. To have the wasted filament
credited back to your deposit, report the failure to the workshop officer
**within 24 hours** of the session, with a photo of the failed part.

Failures reported later than that are not credited, because the filament cannot
be verified after the bin is emptied.

## Finishing up

Clear the build plate, return tools to the board, and take your print with you.
Prints left for more than one week are recycled.

# Membership FAQ

## Who can join?

Membership is open to **all enrolled students**, at any year of study and from
any department. You do not need a technical background, and you do not need to
own any equipment.

Staff and alumni may attend meetings as guests but cannot hold membership.

## What does it cost?

Membership costs **150 TL per academic year**. The fee is the same whether you
join in September or in March — it is not prorated.

The fee covers meeting costs, consumables, and part of the equipment budget.

## Can I get a refund?

Yes, within the **first 30 days** of joining. After 30 days the fee is
non-refundable. Refunds are processed by the treasurer and usually take about a
week.

## When do you meet?

The club meets **every Thursday at 18:00 in Room B204**. Meetings run for roughly
two hours and are open to guests. There is no meeting during official university
holidays.

## Can I borrow equipment?

Yes. Members can borrow club laptops for **two weeks** at a time. A refundable
deposit applies. Full conditions, including extensions and late fees, are in the
equipment loan policy.

The 3D printer is booked separately and has its own rules, including a required
safety induction.

## How do I cancel?

Email the treasurer. Membership ends at the end of the current academic year;
there is no mid-year cancellation other than the 30-day refund window.

## Who do I contact?

Bring questions to any Thursday meeting, or email the committee. The committee
answers within a few days during term time, and more slowly over the summer.

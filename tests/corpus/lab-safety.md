# Lab Safety and Induction

Everyone who enters the workshop, for any reason, must have completed a lab
induction. There are no exceptions and no "just watching" visits.

## Induction sessions

Induction sessions are held on the **first Monday of each month at 10:00** in the
workshop itself. Sessions last about ninety minutes. No booking is needed — turn
up on the day.

If you miss a session, the next one is the following month. The club does not run
individual inductions on request.

## How long an induction lasts

A completed induction is valid for **12 months** from the date of the session.
After that it expires and must be repeated before you may enter the workshop
again. Expiry is not waived for members who use the space regularly.

## Rules inside the workshop

- **Closed shoes** are required at all times. Sandals and open shoes are refused
  at the door.
- **No food or drink** anywhere in the workshop, including sealed bottles.
- Long hair must be tied back, and loose sleeves rolled up, around any moving
  machinery.
- Never work alone. At least two inducted people must be present.

## Emergency stop

Every machine can be cut off using the **red emergency stop button beside the
workshop door**. It cuts power to the whole room. Use it without hesitating — a
stopped job is cheaper than an injury.

## Reporting incidents

Any incident, injury or near miss must be reported to the **lab coordinator
within 24 hours**. This includes incidents where nothing was damaged and nobody
was hurt. Reports are used to improve the rules, not to assign blame.

## First aid

The first aid kit is mounted on the wall to the left of the door. If it has been
used, tell the lab coordinator so it can be restocked.

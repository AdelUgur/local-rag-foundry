# Equipment Loan Policy

The club lends laptops to members for coursework and project use. This policy
covers laptops and accessories only. Fabrication equipment such as the 3D
printer is covered by a separate guide.

## Who can borrow

Any member with a valid, paid-up membership may borrow a laptop. Borrowing is
not open to non-members or to visitors, even when accompanied by a member.

## Loan period

The standard loan period is **14 days**, counted from the day of collection.

A loan may be extended **once**, by a further **7 days**. The extension request
must reach the equipment officer **before day 12** of the original loan. Requests
made after day 12 are refused, and the laptop must be returned on time.

## Deposit

A refundable deposit of **500 TL** is required for every laptop loan. The deposit
is returned in full when the laptop is handed back undamaged and with all its
accessories.

If the laptop is returned damaged, the repair cost is deducted from the deposit.

## Late returns

Laptops returned after the due date incur a late fee of **25 TL per day**. The
late fee is deducted from the deposit. A member with two late returns in one
academic year loses borrowing rights for the remainder of that year.

## Collection and return

Laptops are collected from and returned to **Room B204**, on weekdays between
**13:00 and 16:00**. Bring your student card. Nothing is handed over without it.

## Condition on return

Please return laptops charged and with personal files removed. The equipment
officer wipes and re-images each machine between loans, so anything left on the
device will be lost.
